## 📋 About

SecurityX is a technology company specialized in **server moderation infrastructure** for the Minecraft ecosystem. We develop and maintain a comprehensive platform that unifies ban management, reporting, appeals, malware scanning, and staff coordination into a single, secure system.

Founded by server operators who were tired of juggling half a dozen tools just to keep their communities clean, SecurityX was built from the ground up with **real-world moderation workflows** in mind. Every feature exists because someone needed it.

---

## 🎯 Mission

> **To make fair play the default.**

We believe that every player deserves a level playing field. Cheating, exploiting, and toxic behavior don't just ruin individual games — they erode the trust that holds communities together. Our mission is to give server staff the tools they need to protect that trust, efficiently and effectively.

## 🔬 Our Approach to Anti-Cheat

### Philosophy

We don't build anti-cheat modifications that run inside the game client. Instead, we focus on the **infrastructure layer** — the tools that server staff use to detect, investigate, and act on rule violations.

| Approach | What It Means |
|:---|---:|
| 🧩 **Evidence-based** | Every action is supported by logs, reports, and player history |
| 👥 **Staff-centric** | Tools are designed for human moderators, not automated bots |
| 🔗 **Integrated** | Discord, in-game, and web all feed into the same system |
| 📈 **Data-driven** | Patterns and trends inform moderation strategy |
| 🛡️ **Secure by design** | The platform itself resists abuse and tampering |

### Why It Works

Cheaters evolve. Anti-cheat clients that rely on signature detection fall behind. But a **well-organized staff team with great tools** will always win — because human judgment, backed by comprehensive data and instant action capabilities, adapts faster than any static detection.

SecurityX gives your team the **command center**, not the weapon.
